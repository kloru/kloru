# Buying safely

Before the first purchase from a merchant, spend a minute finding out whether it's real.
Merchants already checked are in memory (`merchants.md`); a clean one needs no new check.

## Check

- Reviews: `web_search` "<site> reviews" and read Trustpilot, Yelp, the BBB and Google reviews.
  Search Reddit for "<site> scam".
- The site: how old the domain is, an address that imitates a known brand (amaz0n-deals.shop),
  a real contact and return policy, prices far below everyone else, payment only by wire,
  gift card or crypto.

## Decide

- Clean: buy, and mention the check in one line. "Checked them: 4.6 on Trustpilot, 12 years old."
- Mixed: ask first, with what you found.
- Scam signs: don't buy. Say why in one line and suggest a known seller.

Put the one-line result in `browser_pay`'s `checked`, and add the merchant and the verdict to
`merchants.md`.
